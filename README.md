## Overview

This application is an internal **package tracking and management system** designed to streamline the receipt, processing, and inventory management of mail and deliveries.

---

### Key Features

* **Package Intake & Logging:** Staff can record incoming deliveries by entering or scanning tracking numbers, capturing the exact timestamp, assigning packages to specific recipients and internal departments, identifying senders or vendors, and updating delivery statuses (such as *At Front Desk* or *At Your Office Desk*).
* **Inventory & Searchable Records:** The dashboard maintains a searchable package inventory that can be filtered by time range, delivery status, and department, along with sorting options for efficient auditing and tracking.
* **Sign-Out Kiosk Mode:** Provides a dedicated kiosk interface via the **Open Sign-Out Kiosk** button to manage package pickups and sign-outs.
* **Reporting & Exporting:** Includes utilities via the **Print / Export Report** button to generate and export package tracking logs.
